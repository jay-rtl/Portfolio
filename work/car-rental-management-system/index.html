Car Rental Booking & Management System
01 / Overview

Built around a business need.

This custom WordPress booking and management platform was created for car rental businesses that need more than a basic booking form. It connects the customer booking experience with fleet availability, payments, customer records, rental operations, and staff management.

02 / The challenge

What needed to work better.

Traditional rental websites often separate the public booking experience from the actual operation. Reservations may arrive through calls, messages, spreadsheets, or simple forms while availability and payments are managed manually.

03 / The solution

A considered approach.

I developed a custom WordPress plugin that provides both the customer booking workflow and a dedicated rental-management workspace for vehicles, reservations, customers, availability, payments, staff activity, and reports.

Technical implementation

A WordPress Plugin Built Like a Business Application

This project goes beyond a standard booking form. The custom plugin handles operational logic around vehicle information, availability, booking states, customer records, payments, user responsibilities, validation, and responsive front-end workflows.

Outcome

One connected workflow for customer reservations and the internal work required to manage a rental from booking through vehicle return.
