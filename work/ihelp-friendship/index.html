iHelp Friendship
01 / Overview

Built around a business need.

A humanitarian organization website presenting ihelpfriendship's community work in Bohol, from support for students and seniors to education and awareness initiatives.

02 / The challenge

What needed to work better.

Introduce the organization's story and community initiatives clearly while helping visitors discover its work and ways to support it.

03 / The solution

A considered approach.

A photography-led website connecting the organization's story, vision, community programs, project gallery, and support information through clear navigation and calls to action.

Key features

Useful in practice.

  • Organization story, mission, and vision
  • Community program presentation
  • Projects and photography gallery
  • Support and participation pathways
  • Responsive navigation
  • Editorial typography and image-led storytelling
Technical implementation

The tools behind the experience.

This project combines HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Responsive Design with a responsive content structure and a customer-focused interface. The implementation supports the features listed above and the website experience shown in the preview.

Outcome

A clear digital home where visitors can understand the organization's work, explore community initiatives, and find ways to get involved.
